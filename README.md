🏛️ KaalDarshan

Experience History. Don't Just Read It.

KaalDarshan is an immersive heritage experience that uses **Augmented Reality (AR), 3D visualization, and location-aware technology** to bring historical events and cultural heritage to life.

Instead of simply reading about history on a board or webpage, KaalDarshan allows users to experience historical scenes within the context of the actual heritage location.

---

🌟 Vision

India has thousands of historically significant monuments and sites, but much of their history is experienced through static boards, photographs, and written descriptions.

KaalDarshan aims to transform heritage exploration into an **interactive and immersive experience**.

> **Walk into history. See the past come alive.**

---

## 💡 How KaalDarshan Works

KaalDarshan combines:

- 📍 Location-aware experiences
- 📱 Mobile AR camera
- 🧊 3D historical environments and characters
- 🎭 Interactive historical scenes
- 📚 Verified historical information
- 🏛️ Heritage-site specific storytelling

The experience changes according to the heritage site and the historical event associated with it.

### Example

A visitor standing at a historically significant location can open KaalDarshan and point their camera toward the relevant area.

Instead of seeing only the present-day structure, the application can overlay a reconstructed historical scene onto the real environment.

This creates a connection between:

**REAL LOCATION → HISTORICAL CONTEXT → AR EXPERIENCE**

---

## 🏛️ Initial Heritage Experiences

### 🇮🇳 Jallianwala Bagh

An immersive reconstruction of the historical context surrounding the Jallianwala Bagh tragedy.

The experience focuses on historical storytelling while preserving respect for the significance of the site.

### 👑 Mysore Palace

An interactive royal-era experience that can recreate aspects of the palace's historical environment, royal ceremonies, architecture, and cultural heritage.

### 🛕 Hampi

An immersive exploration of the Vijayanagara-era heritage of Hampi through 3D reconstruction and contextual AR storytelling.

---

## 🧠 Core Concept

KaalDarshan is not intended to be a generic AR application.

The historical experience is **context-dependent**.

The location, orientation, and selected historical scenario determine what the user experiences.
