Discover the Experience → How it works
The idea

History should not stay behind glass.

Traditional heritage interpretation tells us what happened. KaalDarshan is designed to let visitors understand where it happened and visualize the context in the very space where history unfolded.

01

Location-aware

The experience is tied to the heritage site and its meaningful spatial points—not a generic historical video.

02

Spatial AR

Historical characters, objects and scenes are designed to appear within the visitor's physical environment.

03

Source-grounded

Historical narratives are built from verified sources and organized into a structured knowledge base.
